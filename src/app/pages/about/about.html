Who We Are

Star Media Group Berhad (SMG) is one of Malaysia's most trusted and established media organisations. Having evolved beyond our print heritage, we are now a fully integrated, multi-platform media company spanning print, digital, radio, events, and content marketing — connecting audiences across every touchpoint.

We uphold journalistic excellence through award-winning work that reflects our commitment to credibility, depth, and public trust, while building a future-ready organisation — one that not only reflects society, but helps shape it for the better.

Our Focus
